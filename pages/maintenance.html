General Lawn Maintenance in Calgary

A great-looking yard is more than just mowing. It's the clean edge along the driveway, the trimmed shrubs that aren't encroaching on the walkway, the flower beds that aren't overrun with grass, the corners that a standard mow misses. That's what general maintenance covers.

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages for homeowners who want their yard to look sharp all season without lifting a finger. We handle edging, string trimming, bed weeding, shrub trimming, and all the detail work that separates a maintained yard from a neglected one.

General maintenance works best as an ongoing service. We assess your property on the first visit and build a scope of work that keeps everything looking consistently clean throughout the season — not just the day we visit.

✓

Edging along all hard surfaces — crisp, defined lines along driveways, walkways, patios, and curbs

✓

String trimming — every spot the mower can't reach — fences, posts, garden beds, and corners

✓

Garden bed weeding — keep beds clean and defined so they frame the lawn instead of fighting it

✓

Shrub and hedge trimming — tidy up overgrowth and shape hedges to keep things looking intentional

✓

Flexible scope — we work with what your property needs — nothing more, nothing less
