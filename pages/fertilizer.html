Lawn Fertilization in Calgary

Calgary's soil is notoriously deficient in key nutrients — particularly nitrogen, which is responsible for the deep green colour and vigorous growth most homeowners want. Without regular fertilization, even a well-watered and mowed lawn will slowly thin out, fade, and lose ground to weeds.

We build a custom fertilization plan based on your lawn's grass type, current condition, and the season. We apply slow-release granular fertilizers that feed your lawn steadily over 6–8 weeks, rather than delivering a quick surge that leads to overgrowth and burnout.

We typically recommend 3 applications per season: an early-season application in May to kick-start growth, a mid-season top-up in July, and a late-season feeding in September that helps the lawn store energy for winter and come back stronger in spring.
