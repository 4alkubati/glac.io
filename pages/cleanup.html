Spring & Fall Cleanup in Calgary

Calgary winters are hard on lawns. By the time spring arrives, your yard is often covered in matted leaves, dead grass, twigs, and soil that's been compacted under months of snow and ice. Skipping the cleanup means your lawn is trying to grow through all of that debris — and it will lose.

Our spring cleanup removes everything that's been sitting on your lawn over winter, dethatch the surface layer, and leaves you with a clean slate for the growing season. We also do a final mow and edge to get things looking sharp.

Fall cleanup is just as important. Leaving leaf cover on the lawn through winter creates matted layers that suffocate the grass underneath and trap moisture that leads to disease. We clear everything before the first snow so your lawn can go into winter clean.

✓

Full debris removal — leaves, dead grass, sticks, and anything else that accumulated over the season

✓

Surface dethatching — loosen the top layer so the new growth has a clear path

✓

Final mow and edge — leave everything looking clean and ready

✓

Bed cleanup included — we clear the garden beds and borders while we're at it

✓

Spring and fall scheduling available — book both at a discounted bundle rate
