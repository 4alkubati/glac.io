Power Rake

Over time, dead grass and organic debris build up between the soil and your lawn — this is called thatch. When thatch exceeds half an inch, it blocks water, air, and nutrients from reaching the roots. Power raking uses a mechanized rake to break up and remove this layer, leaving your lawn ready to absorb everything it needs for a strong growing season. Best done in early spring.

Core Aeration

Calgary's clay-heavy soil compacts under foot traffic and freeze-thaw cycles, choking roots and blocking drainage. Core aeration pulls small plugs of soil out of the ground across your entire lawn, opening pathways for water, air, and nutrients to reach deep into the root zone. The result is a denser, more drought-resistant lawn. Pairs perfectly with fertilization immediately after aeration.
