Our Story

Calgary's Trusted Lawn Care Team

Greenland Lawn Care was founded by Mohammed Nabil with one clear mission: to create and maintain beautiful, healthy lawns that enhance the beauty of every property we serve.

We started small — doing what we do best, showing up on time, and leaving every yard cleaner than we found it. Word spread fast. Today we serve homeowners across Calgary's NW, NE, SW, and SE quadrants.

We're not a franchise or a big corporate operation. We're a local team that actually cares about the quality of our work — and it shows in every mow, every cleanup, and every customer review.
